# Summary: paper-v1-laptop

Generated by `cmd/summary` from the files beside it. Each ratio is a format's
number divided by JSON's on the same case; a cell is the geometric mean of those
ratios over the cases where both have one, with that count in parentheses.
A case where the format writes 0 bytes has a ratio of 0, which a geometric mean
cannot hold: it is left out of the mean and counted as "and N at 0".
Below 1 is smaller or faster than JSON. Regimes, engines and machines are never
mixed in one number.

## For the paper

`tables/` holds the same numbers as LaTeX: one `tabular` per table, the inline
numbers as `\resultdef{key}{value}` lines in `numbers.tex`, and the figures'
data as `.dat` files. `paper/main.tex` reads them; nothing there is typed in.
