# Public BI: the first rows against the seeded sample

Not a result of the paper. It is the evidence for the change of subset rule
recorded in `datasets/README.md` ("Subset history"): until 2026-11-02 each table's
subset was its first 16 384 rows; since then it is a seeded simple random sample
of 16 384 rows (bottom-k on splitmix64, seed 20261102).

How it was produced: `TestSizes` over `publicbi/*` twice, with the same build
(the types regenerated by `typesgen` from the sample), once with `COLBIN_DATA`
pointing at a directory whose `publicbi/` is `datasets/data/publicbi-head/`, then
`datasets/publicbi/head_vs_sample.py HEAD/sizes.csv SAMPLE/sizes.csv`.

Caveat: the types' `pb:"sint"` tags follow the sample. Four head columns hold
negatives the sample does not (Eixo, MulheresMil and Uberlandia `DataDeInicio`,
YaleLanguages `DISCHARGEDATE`), so protobuf's head size for those tables is
slightly larger than with tags fitted to the head.

Change is sample against head: a positive change means the sample is larger.
